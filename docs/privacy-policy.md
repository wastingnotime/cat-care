# Cat Care privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026.

Cat Care is provided by Wasting No Time, operated by Henrique Riccio
Desenvolvimento de Software Ltda. This policy covers the Android app
`org.wastingnotime.catcare` distributed through Google Play.

## Information on your device

Cat Care works offline without an account. Cat names, coat selections, optional
birth and adoption dates, planned care, reminders, notes, measurements, care
history and comments are stored on your device. The app does not send this
information to Wasting No Time. It has no advertising, product analytics,
tracking SDKs or cloud synchronization service.

Notifications are scheduled locally. Notification permission is optional.
Language, appearance and reminder preferences are also stored locally.

## Backups, exports and sharing

You can export your records or choose a folder for automatic daily backups
using Android's document picker. Automatic backups retain the seven newest
copies. New full backups include care records, language, appearance, reminder preferences,
and backup folder/status. Restoring after reinstall may require granting access
to the remembered folder again. Notification permission is managed by Android. Backups and exports may
contain private information, so choose a secure destination. Your chosen
Android document provider may store or synchronize files with its own service.
Its privacy policy applies to that processing.

Sharing a history entry opens Android's share chooser. The recipient or app you
select receives the text you choose to share. Cat Care does not send it to us.
Android device backup and automatic device transfer are disabled for app data.

## Diagnostics

Cat Care contains no Crashlytics or other developer-operated diagnostics SDK.
Google Play and Android may provide Android vitals reports when users choose
to share usage and diagnostics with Google through their device settings.
Google's privacy policy governs those platform services.

## Deleting your information

Use Data in the app to delete one cat's records or all local care data. Clearing
app storage through Android Settings or uninstalling also removes local app
data. These actions do not remove exported files or backups. Delete those
separately from their selected folder and any cloud document provider.
Losing the device or uninstalling without a usable backup can permanently
remove your local records.

## Policy updates and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. For questions
about Cat Care or this policy, contact <hriccio@wastingnotime.org>.
